Three homes in Williamsburg, two duplexes and a triplex, stacked in a terracotta-clad building two blocks from the East River. Of Possible paired the neighbourhood's loft-style living with outdoor space for every unit, and gave the back of the building, so often an afterthought in New York, as much care as the street front.
