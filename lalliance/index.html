Le Skyroom sits at the very top of L'Alliance New York's Beaux Arts building, a short walk from Central Park. Of Possible's renovation clears the room back to its soaring floor-to-ceiling windows, so the city's daylight falls across white oak floors and bright white walls, a calm, open room held high above Manhattan.
