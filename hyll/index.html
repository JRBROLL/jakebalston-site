Hyll is a hotel in Charingworth, near Chipping Campden, in the north Cotswolds: a Grade II-listed manor house and its stone outbuildings, set in sixty acres of meadow with long views across the hills. YOUTH, working with Tim Groom Architects, took the house back rather than adding to it, leaving stone, timber and plaster to carry the rooms, with paintings by contemporary artists hung throughout. An ongoing commission, photographed through the seasons.
