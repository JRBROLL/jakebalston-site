A 1964 modernist house in Ditchling, inside the South Downs National Park, looking over a garden of ponds and mature trees. DEDRAFT added a new heart to the plan and a wing that wraps the garden, staying true to the original language of the house, with bespoke ceramic tiles on a curving entrance wall and Douglas fir lining the rooms.
