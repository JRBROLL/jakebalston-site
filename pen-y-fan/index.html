Pen y Fan

Jake Balston · Commissions · Journal · Information
Location
Pen y Fan, Wales
Year
2017

Corn Du first, then on to Pen y Fan, deep in December snow. The wind, spiteful, swept across the ridge and took the snow with it, lifting it off the slopes and sending it dancing along the mountainside in waves. We followed three figures ahead of us, there and gone again, matchsticks on a white, lightly brushed canvas.
