A 1960 house by the modernist architect Trevor Dannatt, a British take on California's Case Study Houses, saved from possible demolition. Coppin Dockray peeled back decades of piecemeal additions, added black steel extensions and new gardens for a growing family, and cut the house's annual carbon emissions by more than half.

The careful balance between the building's character and form. Sandra Coppin, Coppin Dockray
