Firefly Adventures

Jake Balston · Commissions · Journal · Information
Location
Whernside, Yorkshire Dales
Year
2025

Somewhere in the middle of a Firefly Adventures programme, the group conversation and self-reflection step outside and onto a hill. For this group it was Whernside, climbing from the Ribblehead Viaduct into wind and rain that never eased. I followed behind with the camera: hoods up, heads down, the path vanishing into cloud ahead and reappearing at our feet.

Sideways rain, cutting wind, biting at the nose, warm smiles, powerful words, carry the weary legs. Jake Balston
