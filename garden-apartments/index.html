76A Cooper Street is a new five-storey building in Bushwick, Brooklyn, with four apartments. At the back the building steps down in a run of terraces, and each one carries a small garden for the home beside it, so every apartment, however high, lives at garden level. To the street it keeps a sober face of corrugated metal, plain and built to last.

Each unit is effectively a garden level apartment. Of Possible
