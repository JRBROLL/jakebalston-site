A museum for Lakeland Arts on the eastern shore of Windermere, rehousing an internationally significant collection of boats on the site of the old steamboat museum. Carmody Groarke reworked the Lake District's working buildings into a cluster of copper-clad forms, with galleries, a boatyard and a conservation workshop sitting side by side on the water's edge.
