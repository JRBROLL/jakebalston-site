Clifton Cathedral

Architect
Percy Thomas Partnership
Location

Year

Wandering around Clifton, following my feet, I stumbled across the cathedral, tucked away behind the trees. I explored it slowly and alone, the empty holy space blanketed by soft light. Completed by the Percy Thomas Partnership in 1973, it was among the first cathedrals designed around the reforms of the Second Vatican Council, gathering everyone close around the altar.
