Architecture MasterPrize, Winner 2024

A family house at the edge of Mashomack Preserve on Shelter Island, named after the ospreys that nest across the island. In the local 'upside-down' tradition, the living spaces sit upstairs facing the marsh and Cedar Island Cove, while charred cypress cladding, close in colour to beach driftwood, lets the house settle quietly into the tree line.

An architectural threshold at the edge of Mashomack Preserve. Desai Chia
