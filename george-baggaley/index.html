A collaborative morning in George's Sheffield studio, photographing his pots in the light from a single window, with dried flowers from my hometown florist, Little Perth. We talked back and forth about the beauty of well used things, and an inherited stool said it best. A perch from his family's pottery factory, well worn by the generations of potters before him and much loved, with a patina only time can give. George carries that line on in his own way, coiling each vessel by hand from foraged and recycled clay.

Slow conversation, slow light, and a stool sat upon by generations. Jake Balston
