Late sun at Love Trails, the running and music festival on the Gower. Josh had brought his sign, a white board that filled over the weekend with notes from runners passing by. We sat beside it and spoke deeply about photography, running and poetry, and about slowing down enough to notice what is true to you: the idea at the heart of his work as a poet and ultrarunner from Melbourne, and of his project A Note to the Runners. Shot on 35mm film.

There were many more days we were strangers than friends,
but the ones we were friends,
mattered the most. Jake Balston
