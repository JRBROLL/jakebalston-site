Chatham Residence

Jake Balston · Commissions · Journal · Information
Architect
Desai Chia
Location
Chatham, New York
Year
2025
Recognition
World Architecture Festival, Finalist 2026

A single-storey house on 80 acres of Columbia County farmland, for a couple starting a new life away from New York City. It sits on the crest of the land with glass facing the forest and the Catskills, under a curving metal roof drawn from the outline of native oak leaves, whose deep overhangs cast shifting, leaf-like shadows.
