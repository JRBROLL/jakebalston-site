A new family house on a compact suburban lot minutes from downtown Bethesda, balancing openness with privacy among close neighbours. Crisp fibre cement panels give it a sculptural presence that catches the changing light, while a three-storey faceted light well draws daylight deep into the house.

A crisp, sculptural facade that captures texture and shifting light throughout the day. Desai Chia
